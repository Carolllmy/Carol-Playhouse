A place before a story.

This is an early visual study for Living Derry, developed through a design discussion between Codex and Claude. It explores scale, atmosphere and moving through a familiar street. It is not a verified canonical map, a complete game, or a recreation of an event from the novel.

What to try

Drag to look around. Walk with WASD; arrow keys move forward, backward or sideways. “Take a stroll” follows a short route. Compare afternoon with rainy dusk from the same spot. Sound starts only when you switch it on. Press H to hide the interface, and H again to restore it.

The current slice

Study 05 adds a front-yard setback, a longer approach to the porch, a lower neighboring cottage, planted borders, opposite pavement and a layered pine backdrop. Cream trim and darker sashes bring out the authored house’s detail. These are supporting visual assets, not verified Derry buildings.

How it stays light

The scene uses compressed geometry, shared plant instances, batched lawn blades, baked contact shading and a static shadow map. Distant pines use three transparent views rendered from a detailed CC0 tree; they have limited parallax and stay behind the houses. Gutter water shares a 512-pixel reflection updated at 10 Hz in power saver and 20 Hz in smooth mode. It approximates shallow water, rather than simulating fluid.

The browser starts with a 30 fps cap. The display reports draw calls, triangles and the selected cap, not measured GPU frame rate or energy use. The separately labeled lighting study is an offline render of the exported assets. Full baked global illumination and mature deciduous street trees are still unfinished.

The paper boat — playable street

The current scene has a walkable Three.js street, hollow storm drain, folded 3D boat, photographed leaf surfaces, rain, planar water reflections, crouching and a continuous boat-to-drain encounter. A new generated portrait sits on a depth-shaped mesh with eye, breathing and head movement; the final approach cuts directly to black. It is not a fully rigged character. Water uses a lightweight visual effect, not fluid simulation. The earlier cinematic study remains available separately.

Additional curb material: Rough Concrete — Poly Haven, CC0. Boat paper material: Paper 001 — ambientCG, CC0. Spatial drain sound combines a filtered copy of the credited rain recording with sparse synthesized drip accents.

Direction references: the licensed novel excerpt, the 2016 screenplay draft, and American Cinematographer’s discussion of eye lighting. The viewpoint and timing are an independent interpretation. Claude collaborated on camera, water continuity and sound staging.
