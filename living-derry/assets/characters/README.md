# Characters

`xbot.glb`: placeholder mannequin for the street residents. Adobe Mixamo "X Bot" with idle, walk and gesture clips, redistributed in the three.js r186 examples (`examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb`). Mixamo characters and animations are royalty-free for use in projects; they may not be redistributed as standalone assets.

To swap in period-dressed people, change `CHARACTER` in `npc-town.js` (model URL, clip names, head bone, material patterns). Nothing else needs to change.
