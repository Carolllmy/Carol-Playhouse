Release the boat, then choose Follow the boat to walk alongside it. Stop following, WASD, or dragging the view returns control to you. You can also walk along the curb yourself. Following automatically flows into kneeling and the encounter. The final approach cuts to pure black. Press Escape at any time to restart, or tap the black screen after the ending.

Independent playable interpretation. The environment and boat are 3D. In this public build nothing resolves in the dark of the drain. No connection to Bill’s gameplay state.
